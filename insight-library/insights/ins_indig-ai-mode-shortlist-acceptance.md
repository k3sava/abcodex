# AI Mode collapses the product consideration set to a shortlist 88% of users accept without checking

## Claim
AI Mode collapses ecommerce product discovery to a pre-curated shortlist that 88% of users accept without checking, making top-3 placement and brand recognition inside the shortlist the decisive organic rank factors.

## Mechanism
Classic search returned ten or more results and pushed comparison onto the user. AI Mode returns a shortlist of three to five products and frames it as a recommendation, not a list. Users treat that framing as authority. Near-zero clicking confirms they form preferences inside the AI interface rather than by visiting sites. Rank position within the shortlist compresses dramatically: the top item captures 74% of selections while rank 3 and below takes only 10%. Brand recognition, cited in 34% of AI Mode sessions versus 23% in classic search, operates as a trust proxy inside the shortlist rather than as a driver of direct navigation.

## Conditions
Holds when: AI Mode is the primary product discovery entry point; users have moderate category familiarity and trust AI curation. Fails when: buyers have high prior knowledge and use AI Mode only for price comparison rather than discovery; high-consideration purchases where the cost of a wrong choice pushes users to verify independently.

## Evidence
Growth Memo's AI Mode user behavior study, October 2026:

"88% of participants in our AI Mode" user behavior study "took the shortlist as-is." 74% of participants picked the top-ranked product; rank 3 and below received only 10% of selections. 64% of users clicked nothing on average. Brand recognition was cited as a trust signal in 34% of AI Mode sessions versus 23% in classic search, and only 23% of users visited an external site directly.

## Signals
- Category-level organic traffic drops while brand query volume holds or grows.
- Conversion rate per organic session rises because sessions that do arrive are post-shortlist validations, not discovery visits.
- Top-3 shortlist inclusion correlates more strongly with sales lift than raw keyword ranking.

## Counter-evidence
The study does not report sample size or methodology in full, so the 88% figure may not generalize across categories or demographics. Classic search behavior studies varied widely by category; AI Mode acceptance rates likely vary the same way. Indig presents his own data, which has not been independently replicated.
