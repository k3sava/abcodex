# Email is a floor channel because owned relationships hold when organic search drops

## Claim
Email outperforms organic search over a two-year period of search traffic decline because email relationships are owned and compound independently of algorithm changes, while organic traffic is a rented channel subject to sudden platform-level erosion.

## Mechanism
Organic search traffic flows through a third-party algorithm. When Google increases ad density, deploys AI Overviews, or changes ranking signals, organic traffic drops immediately and without recourse. Email subscribers, once opted in, stay on the list. That difference in ownership is the structural cause of the divergence: email grew +16.3% US and +17.0% France year over year (January to August 2026 vs. 2025) while organic traffic fell 13% US and 24% France over two years. No organic algorithmic shift affects email volume. Indig frames the root cause as attribution avoidance: "Blaming every lost click on AI is a go-to avoidance mechanism." Multiple forces drove organic losses (rising ad load, AI Overviews, algorithm changes), but email remained immune to all of them.

## Conditions
Holds when: a site has an existing email list it actively grows; email adds value that justifies subscription and continued opens.
Fails when: a site has no email program and cannot build one quickly; or when the email volume in Indig's data includes order confirmation and transactional messages, which inflate the growth figure without indicating audience growth.

## Evidence
Indig cited Similarweb data across a sample of US and French websites. Key data points:
- Organic search: -13% US, -24% France (August 2024 to August 2026).
- Email channel: +16.3% US, +17.0% France (January to August 2026 vs. 2025).
- Paid search: +20.8% US, +20.3% France (same period, excluding googleadservices.com).
- Direct traffic: largest channel at 43.5% median share (US), but declined 2.7% US and 3.1% France year over year.
- Site-size split: median small sites held organic flat; median large sites lost 17.6% (US) and 26.0% (France).

> "I see email as severely underrated and a prime audience-building channel."

A reported ChatGPT referrer-tagging bug shifted roughly 31,000 daily visits per day from ChatGPT to Direct across 500 pages, illustrating that attribution data for newer channels is unreliable. Indig caveats that email growth may include functional messages.

## Signals
- Email list size growing while organic traffic falls, without a corresponding drop in email engagement rates.
- Month-over-month email contribution to revenue holding flat or rising while organic contribution falls.
- Year-over-year email acquisition cost per subscriber remaining stable as organic CAC rises.

## Counter-evidence
Email growth data may include transactional and order-confirmation messages that are not audience-building in nature. If the Similarweb sample overweights e-commerce, the email gains may partly reflect a retail-driven category effect (US retail and marketplaces grew email 26.6%). Traffic volume is also not value: a smaller email audience of high-intent subscribers may outperform a large one of low-intent ones, while raw traffic numbers obscure that difference.
