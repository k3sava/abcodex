# Content that survives a brand-swap test is commodity content and becomes Google's primary enforcement target at scale

## Claim
Content that would remain equally useful with a competitor's brand is commodity content. Producing commodity content at scale is now the leading trigger for Google spam enforcement because Google evaluates pages across four quality dimensions (effort, originality, talent or skill, accuracy) that templates cannot satisfy at volume.

## Mechanism
Google's quality assessment requires properties that template-driven production cannot deliver at scale. Effort means time and research specific to the topic. Originality means something the user cannot find identically elsewhere. Talent or skill means judgment and expertise beyond pattern-matching. Accuracy means verified, current information. A template works for 100 pages because human writers adapt it with real judgment. At 10,000 pages, adaptation collapses and every page converges on the same commodity output. The brand-swap test is a proxy for these four dimensions: if the content is interchangeable across brands, none of the four qualities are present. Google now uses AI to detect this pattern at scale, and Gary Illyes confirmed at Search Central Live Barcelona that scaled content is now more of a problem than link spam.

## Conditions
Holds when: the site has grown content programmatically or through AI at high volume; the content targets informational queries where AI answers already satisfy user intent.
Fails when: the content is highly proprietary (original research, first-person experience, primary data); when templates are used only to provide structure while substance remains operator-specific.

## Evidence
Google's spam updates have accelerated in 2026, with the interval between updates shrinking from 92 days to 37 days. The updates explicitly target scaled AI content.

> "If you replaced your brand with a competitor's, would the content still be just as useful?"

Solis also articulates a volume-decay principle: "A useful template for 100 pages might offer much less value across 10,000." The test is not about whether AI was used but whether the resulting content is differentiated.

## Signals
- Your content ranks for a query but the page is functionally identical to five competitor pages
- Traffic dropped during a spam update despite no policy violations you can identify
- Your content scores low on effort and originality when evaluated against Google's E-E-A-T framework

## Counter-evidence
Some commodity content still ranks by matching search intent efficiently. Product category pages, structured data tables, and location pages can pass the brand-swap test and still rank. The enforcement is heaviest on informational content that competes with AI Overviews. Structured or transactional content faces different quality criteria.
