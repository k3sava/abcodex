# UGC platforms are the most consistent AI citation source, holding a floor share across every buyer journey stage

## Claim
UGC platforms (Wikipedia, Reddit, LinkedIn) account for 17.1% of AI search citations overall, more than four times the share of publishers at 4.0%, and this share is stable across all buyer journey stages from awareness to conversion, while review platforms concentrate only at the evaluation stage.

## Mechanism
AI search systems treat community-generated content differently from brand messaging. Indig frames the distinction directly: "A community treats a person as a participant… and a logo as an advertiser." Content originating from genuine community participation carries independent-verification signals. Brand-controlled pages read as advertising and are systematically underweighted by AI citation engines relative to community-contributed content.

A freshness signal amplifies this structural advantage. Eighty-three percent of AI citations for commercial queries come from pages updated in the past 12 months. Community platforms update continuously through user contribution, giving them a durable freshness edge that brand-controlled content rarely sustains at the same breadth.

Review platforms concentrate citations at the buyer evaluation stage (up to 13.2%) because they address the uncertainty that appears at that stage. UGC platforms address uncertainty at every stage, which is why their citation share is floor-like rather than stage-specific.

## Conditions
Holds when: AI citation behavior is analyzed across commercial queries where community content exists (mature categories with active Reddit, Wikipedia, and LinkedIn communities).

Fails when: the product or topic has no meaningful community presence on Wikipedia, Reddit, or LinkedIn, which eliminates the floor advantage. May weaken if AI search providers start weighting fresh, structured brand data directly or if community platform content quality degrades.

## Evidence
Analysis of approximately 35,000 ChatGPT citations for commercial queries, December 2025, US market only.

- UGC platforms: 17.1% of cited domains overall
- Publishers: 4.0% of cited domains (UGC is 4.3x larger)
- Review platforms: 7.4% at awareness stage, 13.2% at evaluation stage (stage-concentrated)
- UGC platforms: 15.1% to 18.2% across all journey stages (consistent floor)
- Wikipedia, Reddit, and LinkedIn compose 99% of UGC citations in this sample
- 83% of AI citations for commercial queries come from pages updated in the past 12 months

> "A community treats a person as a participant… and a logo as an advertiser."

## Signals
- Consistent UGC citation share in AI searches across buying stages, independent of purchase-intent intensity.
- Brand content investment does not improve citation share when the brand presence in communities is low.
- Teams participating in communities as genuine contributors (answering questions, not promoting products) begin generating AI citations on those platforms.

## Counter-evidence
The analysis covers a single month (December 2025) in a single market (US). Citation patterns vary across markets and time as AI search evolves. Wikipedia, Reddit, and LinkedIn account for 99% of UGC citations in this sample, meaning community-building on most other platforms produces minimal measurable AI citation uplift. Growth Memo's analysis uses ChatGPT citation data only; AI Mode, Gemini, and Perplexity show different source-weighting patterns. Brands cannot manufacture authentic community participation, so the strategy requires sustained investment with no guaranteed return.
