# Private AI harness companies reach $100M ARR in under nine months with multiples priced on category position, not growth rate

## Claim
Three private AI harness companies reached $100M ARR in under nine months; their valuation multiples of 50 to 100x ARR reflect category leadership, not growth rate, with the fastest grower pricing near the bottom of the range.

## Mechanism
The private AI market in 2026 prices category position the way the 2021 public market priced growth momentum. Once a company occupies the leading slot in a mission-critical AI workflow category (legal AI, governance AI, enterprise CX agents), the market prices the structural durability of that position rather than its trailing growth multiple. The fastest grower at a given moment is not necessarily the best-positioned to compound that position into a durable infrastructure moat.

Tunguz identifies the logic: multiples in the 25 to 125x ARR range across this cohort, with a January 2026 acceleration visible across Legora, Sierra, and Ramp. The acceleration is attributed to either sustained growth or a favorable fundraising environment, but either way the distribution of multiples within the cohort does not correlate with growth rank. The pattern mirrors the 2021 public market multiple era, with approximately 3x faster underlying growth rates.

## Conditions
Holds when: companies occupy category-leading positions in AI-specific workflows where the switching cost is high and the workflow is mission-critical. Fails when: the category is contested by multiple equally positioned players, or when the AI workflow is peripheral to the buyer's core operations.

## Evidence
Harvey (legal AI) valued at 50x ARR. Legora (governance AI) at 56x ARR. Sierra (enterprise CX agents) at 100x ARR. All three reached $100M ARR in under nine months. The fastest grower in Tunguz's private AI cohort priced near the bottom of the 25 to 125x valuation band. Ramp and Decagon also appear in the analysis, with multiples clustering toward the lower end of the range.

The January 2026 acceleration across Legora, Sierra, and Ramp is visible in the valuation data, suggesting a market-wide repricing of category leaders regardless of individual growth trajectory.

## Signals
- A company in your category raised its most recent round at a multiple significantly above its historical ARR multiple without a proportional growth acceleration.
- The category leader charges for outcomes or workflow completion rather than seats or users.
- Competitor companies in the same category trade at 3 to 5x lower multiples despite similar or faster growth.

## Counter-evidence
Three data points (Harvey, Legora, Sierra) is a thin empirical base for a generalizable claim. The 2021 comparison is instructive but imperfect: public market multiple compression from 2021 highs was severe. If the same correction applies to private AI valuations, category-position multiples could compress independent of the companies' actual performance. Ramp and Decagon appear at lower multiples despite potentially similar category leadership claims, which Tunguz does not fully explain.
