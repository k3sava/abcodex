# A comprehensive About page earns more AI Overview citations than a homepage because AI systems prefer identity content over commercial copy

## Claim
An enterprise brand's About page can generate five times more AI Overview impressions than its homepage when it is written as a comprehensive identity document covering company history, expertise, people, and reputation rather than as a commercial pitch.

## Mechanism
AI citation engines evaluate pages for their ability to answer "who is this company" queries. About pages that cover founding history, team credentials, domain expertise, product track record, and customer proof provide dense identity signals. Homepages and product pages are built for conversion, not for answering identity questions at depth. The AI treats the About page as a primary identity document and the homepage as a commercial entry point. This difference shows up directly in Google Search Console's Gen AI report as a large citation-volume gap between the two page types.

## Conditions
Holds when: the About page covers the company's full history, founding team, expertise, customers, and credentials in substantive prose rather than as marketing copy.
Fails when: the About page is a short boilerplate paragraph, a team photo gallery with no narrative, or a page that mirrors homepage commercial copy.

## Evidence
Lily Ray, working on an enterprise brand's website, found that its About page was the most-cited page in AI Overviews and AI Mode in Google Search Console's Gen AI report, with five times more impressions than the homepage. The page was written for humans, not for AI systems. Her recommendation: make your company's history, expertise, people, products, and reputation easy to find, understand, and verify.

## Signals
- About page impressions in the Google Search Console Gen AI report consistently exceed homepage impressions
- AI Mode outbound links surface the About page for branded category queries even when the homepage ranks higher in organic search

## Counter-evidence
Brands that dominate their category through sheer third-party mention volume can earn strong AI citations without a detailed About page. The About page advantage is most pronounced for brands where AI systems have limited offsite identity material to draw from.
