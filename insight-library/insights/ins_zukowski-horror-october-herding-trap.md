# October is the worst month for serious horror game revenue, not the best

## Claim
Among the 7,019 horror games on Steam analyzed by Zukowski, October had the highest release volume but the third-worst median revenue for developers earning over $100K, behind only December and January.

## Mechanism
Game players do not follow seasonal consumption patterns like film audiences do. A horror film is a two-hour commitment made on a whim during Halloween. A horror game is a multi-week commitment that players schedule regardless of the season. The real revenue driver in October for horror games is Steam's algorithmic promotion during its annual Halloween sale, not sustained buyer demand. This promotion benefits games already in the library or on wishlists, not games launching into a crowded October release slate. Release concentration increases competition for the same algorithm surface area without expanding the buyer pool.

## Conditions
Holds when: the game targets the serious buyer who needs weeks to complete it, rather than the casual seasonal buyer. Fails when: the game is a short horror experience (1-3 hours) that could convert impulse buyers during a Halloween sale.

## Evidence
Zukowski analyzed 7,019 horror games on Steam. October had the highest release volume among all months. But for developers earning over $100K, October ranked as the third-worst month for median revenue, outperformed by June and other months with fewer releases.

Of the nine most successful horror titles, only two launched in October. The rest launched in September (Phasmophobia), April (Buckshot Roulette), May (The Forest), and other months.

> "games are so long. A holiday movie is a quick 2 hour commitment that you do on a whim. But to play a game can take up a whole month."

## Signals
- Horror games releasing in June or September achieve higher median revenue than October peers with comparable wishlist counts
- A shorter horror experience (under 3 hours) converting impulse buyers during the Halloween sale is a different bet, and October may fit it

## Counter-evidence
The Halloween Steam sale gives horror games promotional placement that other months lack. For games with existing wishlists, the sale conversion window can be significant. The aggregated median may obscure breakout titles that timed their release to the promotional wave.
