# AI-era sales reps carry multiples-higher quotas, forcing quarterly comp resets

## Claim
AI tools amplify rep throughput enough that AI-era sales teams stay small while each rep carries quotas many times higher than before; the pace of model-capability change, roughly every 45 days, makes annual compensation planning obsolete.

## Mechanism
AI handles more of the research, outreach, and follow-up work per deal, increasing what each rep can process without proportionally increasing headcount. At the same time, each model generation shifts what deals are winnable, what competitors offer, and how buyers evaluate solutions, compressing the useful planning horizon from a year to a quarter.

## Conditions
Holds when: Infrastructure-layer AI sales, where customers are AI-native companies growing fast and deals can reach eight figures on short cycles.

Fails when: Application-layer AI sales, which more closely resembles prior SaaS motions. Shahali notes that application-layer teams still run account executives alongside customer success managers in a pattern close to traditional software sales. Small AI-era teams also tend to underinvest in outbound prospecting when the team stays lean.

## Evidence
Shahali grew Fireworks AI from roughly $3M to roughly $400M in revenue.

> "Each rep, the quota they can carry is just multiples higher."

> "Now we're setting comp every quarter because things change so much from quarter to quarter."

Infrastructure-layer deals at AI-native customers can close in two weeks at eight-figure values. Competition is intense because every inference provider pursues the same accounts.

## Signals
- Quota-per-rep at AI infrastructure companies exceeds what the same rep carried at prior SaaS roles by a meaningful multiple
- Compensation plans revised more than once a year without a headcount change
- Ramp time for new reps shortens as AI handles the research and prep work that previously required tenure

## Counter-evidence
Small teams generate higher per-rep output but leave outbound prospecting and account expansion underserved. Shahali flags this directly. The 8-figure deal in two weeks is specific to AI-native infrastructure buyers; it does not transfer to enterprise deals with longer legal and procurement cycles. Annual comp plans may persist in regulated industries or where deal structures change slowly.
