Jake Castillo co-founded and serves as COO of Cal AI, a photo-based calorie tracking app. Castillo has documented the company's creator marketing experiments publicly via the Sub Club podcast at RevenueCat, including a costly influencer test with UFC creators that produced no measurable download increase. His frameworks center on the gap between creator audience size and actual conversion intent, with a focus on what the audience is thinking about at the moment they encounter the post.

## Operating themes
- **Audience context over audience size** Creator niche and follower count don't predict conversion; comments reveal whether the audience is in the mindset to act.
- **Viewing context as the conversion filter** Fitness-adjacent creators don't convert for calorie-restriction tools when their audience is in entertainment or indulgence mode.
