Cate Dombrowski. AI search researcher at iPullRank who focuses on behavioral data, cross-platform experimentation, and the measurement gap between how AI search is described and what the data actually shows. Her work combines clickstream panel matching, survey research, and statistical analysis to produce empirical comparisons between Google and AI search behavior at scale.

## Operating themes
- **Behavioral evidence over assumption** Dombrowski runs matched-panel studies comparing the same user across Google and AI surfaces rather than theorizing about how behavior differs.
- **Measurement frame matters** Her research consistently shows that the categories used to measure traditional search do not transfer cleanly to AI search, and that applying the wrong ruler produces misleading conclusions.
- **Surface-specific conditioning** Her findings emphasize that users' search habits are learned responses to what a surface rewards, not stable expressions of preference, and that different surfaces produce measurably different behavior from the same person.
