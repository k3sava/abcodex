Daniel Stenberg. Creator of curl and libcurl, two of the most widely deployed open-source network transfer libraries in the world. Curl ships on billions of devices and in thousands of products. Stenberg has maintained it continuously since 1998, publishing technical post-mortems, security disclosures, and development commentary on his personal blog at daniel.haxx.se.

## Operating themes
- **Living software vs. code at a point in time** The curl project's durability comes from continuous iteration, bug fixes, and security patches, not from the choice of programming language or any single architectural decision.
- **Volume is not quality** Agent-generated code can replicate the form of a codebase quickly; it cannot replicate the institutional knowledge embedded in years of commits, fixes, and behavioral corrections.
- **Community as competitive moat** Curl's resilience against language-based competitors and clones depends on the size, speed, and judgment of its community, not any technical property of the implementation.
