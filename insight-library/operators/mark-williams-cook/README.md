# Mark Williams-Cook

## Bio
Mark Williams-Cook is an independent SEO researcher and consultant best known for building AlsoAsked.com, a tool for mapping People Also Ask data at scale. He is a frequent contributor to Search Engine Journal and focuses on the intersection of search behavior, AI retrieval, and what evidence actually supports versus what practitioners assume.

## Operating themes
- **Operating thesis:** most GEO and AEO tactics lack the counterfactual testing that would distinguish genuine signal influence from coincidental AI system defaults; practitioners mistake neutral facts about how AI systems behave (crawling, indexing, RAG retrieval) for evidence that a tactic changes AI output.
- **Evidence quality in GEO**
- **LLM behavior vs. LLM influence**
- **Crawler and indexing data interpretation**
- **Empirical SEO**
