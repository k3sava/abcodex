Dex Horthy. Founder of HumanLayer, a platform for human-in-the-loop AI agent workflows, and an independent consultant who has worked with roughly 100 production AI engineering teams. Horthy ran a five-month fully automated software factory experiment starting in mid-2025, which failed after four months when an unreviewed primary key bug propagated through the entire codebase. That failure anchored his practical writing on context engineering, loop safety, and the correct roles for human oversight in agentic systems.

## Operating themes
- **Context as the primary engineering variable** Horthy frames context window management as a four-factor problem: size, information quality, missing information, and conversation trajectory, each requiring a distinct intervention.
- **Dumb zone as a production concept** He popularized the "dumb zone" heuristic: models perform worse, not just differently, past the optimal context length, and the degradation can be destructive.
- **Loop safety over full automation** After his dark factory experiment failed, Horthy advocates for a hybrid approach that preserves human review for architectural decisions while reducing it for implementation code.
