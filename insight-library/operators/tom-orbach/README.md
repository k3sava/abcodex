Tom Orbach. Growth marketing operator who built the Viral Post Generator (two million users) and writes the Marketing Ideas newsletter, now at 100,000 subscribers. He leads growth marketing at Google, previously at Wiz. His core thesis is that saturated growth playbooks produce diminishing returns, and that outrageous-by-design tactics capture attention at a fraction of the budget required to buy it.

## Operating themes
- **Outrageous-by-design** Growth tactics that break audience expectations generate organic sharing. Surprise is a force multiplier on creative spend.
- **Word-of-sight marketing** Embedding a brand into users' own public actions converts every user action into a free impression.
- **Milestone-as-growth-loop** Shareable milestones, physical or digital, convert customer achievement into social proof and referrals.
