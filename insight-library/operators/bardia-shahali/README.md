Bardia Shahali is VP of Sales at Granola, the on-device AI meeting notes company. Before Granola, he built the sales function at Fireworks AI from the ground up, growing the company from roughly $3M to roughly $400M in revenue. His work focuses on how AI tools change the economics of sales teams: how many reps a team needs, what quotas are achievable per rep, and how fast compensation plans must adapt as model capabilities change every quarter.

## Operating themes
- **Rep quota amplification.** Shahali argues that AI tools raise what a single rep can carry in quota, keeping teams small while total revenue grows, but also that small teams leave outbound prospecting underserved.
- **Quarterly comp cadence.** At infrastructure-layer AI companies, model capabilities and competitive dynamics shift fast enough that annual comp plans become obsolete; Shahali runs quarterly resets.
- **Infrastructure vs. application sales motion.** He distinguishes sharply between infrastructure AI sales (fast cycles, eight-figure deals, forward-deployed engineers) and application AI sales (closer to traditional SaaS, account executives plus customer success).
