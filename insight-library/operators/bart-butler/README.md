Bart Butler. Chief technology officer at Proton, the Swiss privacy company behind ProtonMail and ProtonVPN. Butler published an internal engineering manifesto in November 2026 that argued the fundamental role of the software engineer had shifted from writing code to directing AI agents. He framed engineering expertise as more valuable than ever, while the act of hand-writing code became something AI handles. His manifesto triggered significant industry commentary, with supporters citing it as an honest operator-level account of where AI-assisted development was heading and critics noting the absence of measured productivity data.

## Operating themes
- **Engineer as AI team leader** The software engineer's value shifts from code production to directing, correcting, and improving AI agents. Each engineer now manages a "direct report" roster of models.
- **Phase 2 transformation** Most teams are stuck optimizing individual tasks with AI (phase 1). The bigger gain comes from rethinking entire workflows: CI, design review, approvals, and code review.
- **Expertise survives the transition** Sound engineering judgment, domain knowledge, and professional discipline remain essential and do not disappear when agents write the code.
