Curtis Anderson. Founder and CEO of Nursa, a healthcare staffing platform. Anderson has built Nursa through a period of rapid AI adoption and publishes on leadership and hiring. His interview methodology focuses on identifying candidates who have survived genuine ambiguity, which he sees as the signal best correlated with performance in an AI-accelerated operating environment.

## Operating themes
- **Near-death as hiring signal** Prior experiences of surviving professional ambiguity predict AI-era performance better than credentials or polished narratives.
- **Iterations as the core variable** Candidates who have had many at-bats in uncertain situations outperform those who have had fewer, cleaner ones.
