# Sophie Alpert

## Bio
Sophie Alpert is an engineer at Clay and former React core team lead at Meta. She writes at sophiebits.com on software engineering, AI workflows, and the practical limits of language model tools. Her post on lossless text transformations was adopted as company writing policy at Clay.

## Operating themes
- **Operating thesis:** language models are useful but structurally unable to preserve author intent through text transformation; teams that treat AI edits as equivalent to human edits are accepting meaning loss they have not accounted for.
