Kyle Kingsbury is the creator of Jepsen, the widely cited tool for testing the correctness of distributed databases under network partitions, crashes, and concurrent operations. Jepsen analyses are primary-source records of how production database systems behave under adversarial conditions: they document what vendors claim, what tests reveal, and where the gaps between those two are. Kingsbury publishes the full analysis and raw data, making his work reproducible and independently verifiable.

## Operating themes
- **Correctness over claims** Testing what a system does, not what its documentation asserts, as the only reliable signal of software quality.
- **LLMs as a documentation credentialing problem** LLM-generated documentation decouples polish from reliability, raising the question of what can serve as a credible proxy for quality.
- **Adversarial testing as trust infrastructure** Making the gap between claim and behavior explicit serves both users and the ecosystem, even when it is commercially inconvenient for vendors.
