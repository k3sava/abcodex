Josh Miller. Co-founder and CEO of The Browser Company, the company behind Arc and Dia. Miller thinks publicly about the right relationship between humans and AI tools, arguing against delegation-first approaches in favor of AI that amplifies human thinking. His product work at The Browser Company reflects a consistent interest in how software can preserve rather than erode human agency.

## Operating themes
- **AI as Socratic prompter** Software that asks questions and surfaces your thinking may produce better outcomes than software that does the work for you.
- **Agency in craft** Retaining the capacity to make decisions and understand your own work matters even when AI can do the work faster.
