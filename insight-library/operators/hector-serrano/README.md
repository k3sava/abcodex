Hector Serrano. Head of Content at Wispr Flow, the AI voice-to-text application valued at $2 billion. Serrano built and runs the company's performance-based UGC program, which scaled to 50 creators producing 500 to 600 paid ads per month. His approach treats creator compensation as a selection mechanism: paying a share of ad spend, not a flat rate, surfaces creators with genuine product affinity and aligns their economics with purchase outcomes. Under his tenure, Wispr Flow reported roughly 30x revenue growth in one year.

## Operating themes
- **Performance-based creator economics** Compensation tied to ad spend share selects for authentic advocates and self-enforces quality without manual curation at scale.
- **UGC as a volume-and-authenticity play** High output volume (500 to 600 ads per month) requires a system, not a campaign mentality; the selection filter is what keeps quality from degrading at scale.
