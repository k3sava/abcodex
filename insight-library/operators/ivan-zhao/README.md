Ivan Zhao. Co-founder and CEO of Notion, the productivity and collaboration platform used by millions of teams. Zhao has led Notion from its early days as a document editor to a platform that integrates AI throughout the product. His public commentary touches on how people relate to AI-generated and human-generated content, and what that distinction means for products built on top of AI.

## Operating themes
- **Human expression as a distinct product** The preference audiences have for work made by other people may persist even when AI can produce equivalent outputs.
- **Consumer of human tokens** People may increasingly seek out each other's perspective and expression as AI makes generic output abundant.
