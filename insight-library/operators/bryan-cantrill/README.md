# Bryan Cantrill

Co-founder and CTO of Oxide Computer Company, which builds rack-scale cloud computers for on-premises deployment. Previously VP Engineering and CTO at Joyent, and before that a Distinguished Engineer at Sun Microsystems where he co-created DTrace, the dynamic tracing framework that became foundational for production observability in Unix-derived systems. DTrace shipped in Solaris 10 in 2005 and influenced tracing infrastructure across macOS, FreeBSD, and Linux.

Cantrill writes and speaks publicly about systems software, hardware-software co-design, and the organizational dynamics of engineering teams. He is a direct critic of AI catastrophism and writes with precision about what physical constraints do and do not bound AI capability claims.
