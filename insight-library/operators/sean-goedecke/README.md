Sean Goedecke. Software engineer and prolific technical blogger at seangoedecke.com. Goedecke writes primarily about software engineering craft, AI tool use, and the structural changes AI is introducing to how engineers work and communicate. His posts are notable for specific, testable claims with explicit conditions rather than broad industry commentary. He publishes frequently (multiple times per week in late 2026) and covers both practical AI workflow topics and philosophical questions about LLM behavior.

## Operating themes
- **AI agency and noticing** Most AI underuse comes from failing to notice delegation opportunities rather than model limitations. The key skill is developing a trigger for recognizing when a task can be handed off.
- **Proxy metrics corrupt goals** Engagement metrics in writing attract the wrong audience. Writers who optimize for clicks displace the careful readers they actually want with casual audiences they do not.
- **LLM behavior and limits** Goedecke publishes detailed arguments about what LLMs can and cannot reliably do, and why naive extrapolations from their capabilities are wrong. He approaches LLM limitations as a software engineer rather than a researcher.
