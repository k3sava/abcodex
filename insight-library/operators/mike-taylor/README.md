Mike Taylor heads evals at Every, the AI-focused media and software company. He runs controlled multi-model experiments on how AI agents discover, retrieve, and recommend content, publishing results in his column Also True for Humans. His work bridges the gap between content strategy and answer engine optimization by treating agent citation behavior as an empirically testable variable rather than a heuristic.

## Operating themes
- **Agents as a distinct audience.** Taylor treats AI agents as a new reader category with different selection criteria from humans, and runs experiments to characterize those criteria precisely.
- **Empirical AEO.** His methodology mirrors A/B testing: large session counts, single-variable changes, model-controlled conditions, and explicit limitation statements.
- **Answer-first content architecture.** His findings consistently favor content structures that state the answer before building context, inverting the curiosity-driven approach that works for human readers.
